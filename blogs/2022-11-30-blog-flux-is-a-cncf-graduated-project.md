Flux has graduated Today is a very exciting day for the Flux community! Flux is now a graduated project in the Cloud Native Computing Foundation and joining the ranks of Kubernetes, Helm, Prometheus and others in this category. Flux History We all worked very hard to make this happen - it is another important milestone in the Flux success story.
