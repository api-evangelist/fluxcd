We are thrilled to announce the release of Flux v2.3.0 ! In this post, we will highlight some of the new features and improvements included in this release. General availability of Flux Helm features and APIs This release marks a significant milestone for the Flux project, after almost four years of development, the helm-controller and the Helm related APIs have reached general availability.
