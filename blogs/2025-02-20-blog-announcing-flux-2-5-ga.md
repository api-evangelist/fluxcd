We are thrilled to announce the release of Flux v2.5.0 ! In this post, we will highlight some of the new features and improvements included in this release. Highlights Flux v2.5 marks a significant milestone in the project’s evolution, we have integrated Common Expression Language (CEL) with the Flux controllers to enable long-awaited features such as custom health checks and webhook receiver filters.
