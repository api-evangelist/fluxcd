On Jul 7, 2016, Peter Bourgon made the initial commit a6fbd68a to iterate on a fresh way to do continuous delivery. Today, that commit is one decade old, and we celebrate 10 years of Flux. “Happy Birthday to Flux, the project that anticipated all of my needs and solves all my problems on a daily basis!” — Kingdon Barrett, Flux Maintainer, DevOps Engineer @ Navteca The early repo shows Peter and Michael Bridgen implementing the fluxd daemon and fluxctl CLI; the repo also contains user stories on why people might want to use flux.
